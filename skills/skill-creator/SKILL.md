---
name: skill-creator
description: Mint a reusable skill from a completed research session. Do not invent a skill that launders unlogged claims.
---

Turn a session that already happened into a skill that can be followed again.

Mint only when a class has a kill rule, a score, and at least one primary instance with a retrieved source.

If the session was a landing of an unlogged leftover, do not mint a second class-skill. Point at the original class repo.
